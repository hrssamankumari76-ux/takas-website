# TAKAS Glue Website

## Overview
**TAKAS** is a Sri Lankan all-purpose glue brand designed for everyday use.  
This website is a **one-page, mobile-friendly site** showcasing Takas products, sizes, features, and contact information. It also allows users to **call directly** or **order via WhatsApp**.

---

## Features
- Responsive design, works on mobile & desktop
- Call buttons that open the phone keypad
- WhatsApp button that opens your WhatsApp Business chat
- Product information and usage instructions
- Brand trust section with company details
- Footer showing **Wickramasinhe Enterprise**

---

## Product Sizes Displayed
- **50ml – Everyday Carry**: Compact, for preschool & quick fixes  
- **100ml – School & Crafts**: Perfect for students and crafts  
- **1L – Household Use**: Ideal for regular home usage  
- **5L – Heavy Use**: Workshops & frequent users  
- **20L – Bulk Supply**: Schools, institutions, resellers  

---

## Special Feature
- **Slim nozzle bottle** for precise application  
- Clean, controlled glue application  
- Less waste and mess
